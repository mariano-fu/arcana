# T1.16 — Storage survival check

Goal: confirm that `navigator.storage.persist()` was granted on her iPhone, and that
after a week of real use all her data is still there.

A record count alone can't catch a wipe that a later sync refilled from the server.
So the check also compares the **phone identifier**: the first 8 characters of
`meta.device_id`, a UUIDv7 created the first time the app runs on a device. A wiped
database gets a new id, and the "desde" date decoded from it would jump forward.

**This repo is public.** Record only counts, dates and versions here. No client names,
no note text, no tailnet hostname. Screenshots stay off the repo.

## 0. Before (Mariano)

- The deployed build includes T1.16: Ajustes shows a "Datos en este teléfono" section
  under Almacenamiento.
- She opens Arcana **from the home-screen icon**, not from a Safari tab. Only the
  installed app gets the longer storage lifetime.
- If T3.8 (sync login) is deployed during the week, she doesn't log in until the day-7
  check is done, so the week tests local storage only. *(Confirmed by Mariano
  on 2026-10-24.)* If she does log in, the phone identifier still shows whether a wipe happened.

## 2. During the week

- Normal use: real readings, notes, payments.
- Don't delete the app, and don't clear Safari's history and website data.
- Note anything unusual in the log below: an iOS update, the phone short on storage, a
  restart, days without opening the app, records she deliberately deleted.

## 3. Day 7 or later

- The same screenshot. Fill in the **Día 7** column.
- Spot checks by hand:
  - Open the oldest client and one old session that has a note; the note text is there.
  - Open one card note; its text is there.

## 4. Pass criteria

All of these must hold:

- Same phone identifier and same "desde" date (no wipe).
- Estado is still "persistente".
- Every count is ≥ its day-1 value, minus the deletions noted in the log.
- Same oldest client and oldest session dates (unless she deleted that record, in the log).
- The spot checks match.

Otherwise it fails. On a failure, record what differs and stop: no fixes in this task.
